About this archive

Photographs and archival descriptions are drawn from the assigned Box B19 dataset. Names, dates and coordinates reflect available archival records and subsequent interpretation. Some locations are approximate or village-level; records without reliably assigned coordinates remain browseable. A map marker does not necessarily indicate the precise camera viewpoint.

Historical imagery layer uses a 2026 student-exported georeferenced mosaic; original photography date and provenance must be confirmed before final citation. Modern basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors. Original photograph sources and positional notes are displayed in individual records. This is a student digital-humanities project.
